EBY’EKYAMA N’OKUZZAPRIVACY AND RECOVERY

Ebiwandiiko byo bya ggwe, buli lunakuYour records are yours, every day

Amannya ga bammemba, ennamba z’essimu ne ID z’ekibiina kyo bisigala mu kibiina kyo. Tufuna kyetaagisa okukola n’okukuuma ebiwandiiko — tetubitunda, tetubisoma, oba tetubigabana. Okuyingira kukoma ku bakungu be ekibiina kyo kyalonda.Your members’ names, phone numbers and ID references stay with your group. We receive what is needed to create and protect the records — we do not sell them, read them or share them. Access is limited to the officers your group appointed.

Ani asobola okulaba ebikwata ku bammemba?Who can see my members’ details?Amannya, ennamba z’essimu ne ID bisigala mu kibiina kyo. Tufuna kyetaagisa okukola n’okukuuma ebiwandiiko by’ekibiina — tetubitunda, tetubisoma, oba tetubigabana. Okuyingira kukoma ku bakungu be ekibiina kyo kyalonda.Names, phone numbers and national ID references stay with your group. We receive only what is needed to create and protect the group’s records — we do not sell them, read them or share them. Access is limited to the officers your group appointed.

If you ever stop, the records are yours to take with you — and your paper book stays valid the whole time.

Bwe kityo tewali ssimu emu ekutte ekibiina.Recovery depends on the backup you keep. So: take a backup every week, and let the treasurer and the secretary each hold a copy. Then no single phone holds the group up.
