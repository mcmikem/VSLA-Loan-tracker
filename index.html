VSLA · SACCO · Investment clubs · Welfare groups

Buli mmemba alabe okutereka kwe.Every member can see their savings.

VSLA UG kitabo kya digito eky’ebibinja by’okutereka mu Uganda. Kiwandiika okutereka, emigabo, looni, welfare, engassi n’enkiiko ku ssimu — ne bw’oba tolina yintaneeti.VSLA UG is a digital record book for savings groups in Uganda. It records savings, shares, loans, welfare, fines and meetings on a phone — even without internet.

Ekibinja kyo kikuuma ssente. VSLA UG ekuuma records.Your group keeps the money. VSLA UG keeps the records.
